How to Play

  • Goal: score as many points as you can from today's 100 letters. Everyone gets the same letters in the same order.
  • Move and drop: tap a column to move the falling letter there, then tap it again to drop it. ◀︎ ▶︎ ▼︎ work too.
  • Make words: 3 or more letters across (left to right only), down or up light up . Diagonal words score double. A pink 2× vowel doubles any word it's in, and they stack.
  • Hold or clear: each new word pauses the game. Clear it for its points now, or Hold it and build a longer word for more (CAR → CARDS). Double tap a held word to clear it, or press and hold it to choose again.
  • Points: each letter has a value, like A, B, C, Deal. Words of 5+ letters get +5 for each extra letter.
  • ⚠️ Final 10: the last 10 letters are a surprise (Next shows ?) and have a gold edge and ★. Any word that uses one scores double.
  • ⛓ Chains: when you clear a word, the letters above drop down. If they land in a new word, it's a chain and scores double. Careful: the drop can also break up a word you were holding.
  • Stars: earn them with points.

  • Game over when the letters stack to the top, or as soon as the last letter lands (if it makes a word, you can still clear that one). Like Scrabble, every letter left on the board takes its points off, and words you're still holding don't score. Clear them before the end!
  • The website version sends an anonymous check-in so we can count players: the day, your score and the game version. Never your name, contacts or location.
